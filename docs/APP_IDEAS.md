# Ten MagicShelf ideas beyond individual creations

1. Shelf profiles: switch between saved layouts for gaming, exercise and a relaxed workspace.
2. Undo placement changes: restore the last stable shelf layout after an accidental move or tracking reset.
3. Native search, favorites and sorting: find creations quickly inside the VR panel rather than browsing every card.
4. Version history and release notes: show what changed before or after an automatic update, with the existing rollback action close by.
5. A health dashboard: explain missing support files, failed services, tracking issues and repair options in plain language.
6. Shelf backup and restore: export layouts, subscriptions and supported saved data for a new headset or recovery.
7. A storage manager: show package, cache and backup sizes and safely reclaim obsolete downloads while keeping a recovery version.
8. Safe startup mode: recover from a faulty creation by opening MagicShelf with creations Off, without losing saves.
9. Framework accessibility controls: larger panel text and hit targets, high contrast and reduced-motion presentation.
10. A diagnostic bundle preview: let testers inspect and redact a support report before choosing to share it, keeping sharing explicit.

Creation-specific settings remain inside each creation. These ideas concern the
launcher, shelf, updates, support and organization rather than new creations.
